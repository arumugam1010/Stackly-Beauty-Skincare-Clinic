Client Preparation

Pre-Appointment Checklist

Ensure optimal clinical outcomes by following these 5 quick prep guidelines before your facial procedure:

  • Discontinue Retinoids: Avoid prescription tretinoin or active retinols 48 hours prior to treatment.
  • Avoid Direct UV: Do not sunbathe or expose skin to harsh tanning rays for 3 days before visits.
  • Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water to assist cellular conductivity during ultrasonic prep.
  • Arrive Bare-Faced: If convenient, arrive with minimal makeup so your diagnostic scan is instantaneous.
  • Bring Current Routine: Mention your current serums so we identify potential ingredient clashes.
